# About This Site
This is my platform for sharing, and my first step away from social media. On social media, a short piece of text and a pile of photos can become a post. Here, instead, I put a lot of words that nobody wants to read. Words can hold the details and emotions of life, recording more of what life looks like. Carefully arranged photos on social media may look dazzling, but they stay in other people's minds for less time than fireworks. If you want to get to know me, I hope this site can help you meet more sides of me, the real me. A blog can also keep going for a very, very long time. Even if one day I can no longer maintain this domain, it will still be on my computer instead of on some server of unknown origin.

**Subscribe to my [RSS](/rss.xml) if you want to keep up with updates!**
> What's RSS? RSS (Really Simple Syndication) is a tool for subscribing to websites. It lets you know when a site has been updated, so you don't have to keep checking. Want to know how to use it? I recommend Wiwi's [article](https://wiwi.blog/blog/you-should-use-rss/).

Also, the entire English interface of this site is translated by an AI agent. I don't want to type everything in English again after finishing the Chinese version. If anything sounds unnatural, please tell me, ~~though I may not fix it~~.
