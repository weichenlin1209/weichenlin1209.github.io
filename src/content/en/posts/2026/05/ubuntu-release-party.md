# Ubuntu Release Party 2026
Information is a world made of 0s and 1s. Have you ever wondered:

Why can someone bring PC games to a handheld console?

Why isn't the Steam Deck running Windows,
but instead choosing to build SteamOS on top of Arch Linux?

And the recent buzz — "raising lobsters" — letting AI agents run tasks on your own computer or in the cloud?
Why do so many tutorials recommend starting with Unix-like operating systems such as Ubuntu, Debian, or macOS?

Linux can also be the starting point for everyday use, creation, gaming, studying, and even deploying AI tools. Its charm lies in giving us more freedom to take control of our computers.
From what the desktop looks like, shortcuts, input methods, all the way to software packages and system settings — you can gradually reshape it into a working environment that truly fits your own intuition.
This event requires no technical background. It answers all your questions about everyday web browsing, office work, and sluggish computers.
Come take a look, and make your computer feel more like your own tool — simpler to use!

Today, let's throw a party, a feast of Linux!
Let this sharing and discussion become our party!
